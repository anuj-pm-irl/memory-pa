Sam Wilson took the loan cart around the Pier 6 ramp in rain. He returned with a useful list of places that pool water and one astonishingly shiny left shoe.

Dana Ortiz and Sam approved the City Lanterns wet-weather rule. Wet crates stay on Pier 6 until the dock lead signs the dry check. The library will use its reserve while a cart waits at the pier. A volunteer will call the front desk before moving the cart, so Dana can update the loan count on the whiteboard.

Lantern loan receipts travel in the red sleeve in the cart's front pocket. The volunteer initials the receipt after each stop and leaves the white-triangle return box closed until the cart is under cover. Sam will show that sequence to the second crew at the next pickup.

Happy checked that the kitchen crate still had its card clipped to the lid. It did. Tony proposed a single colored folder for any future emergencies and was handed the mop.
