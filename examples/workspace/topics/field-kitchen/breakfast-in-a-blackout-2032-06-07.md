Happy Hogan had the tower kitchen wheels locked, the boxes unfolded and a pot of coffee ready before Tony arrived with the words “mobile waffle platform.” The design review took the time required to pour one cup.

Happy will send forty-eight vegetable breakfast boxes to the Red Hook community kitchen. The first batch goes by the library drive. Sam Wilson will call ahead so the kitchen has a volunteer at the door. The kitchen will keep its own tally of boxes handed out and carry any extras into its lunch count.

Pepper asked Happy to put a menu card in the lid of every box. The front will list what the kitchen packed that morning. The back will have the callback number for ingredient questions. He will bring the sample cards to the June 20 handoff dry run. The group left the color system for the dry run.

Tony asked to broadcast the breakfast menu on the tower's sign. Happy pointed out that half the people in the room were looking for a pen. The pen went into the box of blank labels. The waffle platform has been assigned a future so distant that the forecast contains fog.
